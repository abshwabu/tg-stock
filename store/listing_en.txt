FULL DESCRIPTION (Max 4,000 characters):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inventory is a lightning-fast, 100% offline-first inventory management and Point of Sale (POS) system built specifically for retail shop owners, kiosks, mini-markets, and wholesale traders.

Never lose a sale because of network cuts or slow internet. Every single feature—recording sales, managing inventory, issuing receipts, tracking customer debts (Dube), and checking daily profits—works completely offline on your Android device.

KEY FEATURES BUILT FOR RETAIL SUCCESS:

⚡ Fast POS & Cash Register
• Ring up sales in seconds with an easy-to-use touch counter.
• Split and accept local payment methods: Cash, Telebirr, CBE Birr, and Customer Credit (Dube).
• Instant change calculator and printable or shareable digital receipts.
• Support for item barcode scanning using your phone camera.

📦 Accurate Offline Stock & Inventory Control
• Track stockable products, services, and prepared consumable goods with composite recipes (e.g. injera packs made from teff).
• Real-time low-stock alerts before you run out of essential products.
• Record stock-in purchases, supplier costs, and track spoilage/damage adjustments.
• Manage multiple inventory locations: easily transfer stock between your Main Store and Warehouse without discrepancies.

📒 Customer Credit & Debt Ledger (Dube / ዱቤ)
• Keep accurate records of regular customers who take items on credit.
• Track outstanding customer balances, payment due dates, and custom credit limits.
• Record partial payments, track payment history, and share debt reminder summaries directly via Telegram or SMS.

📊 Daily Profits & Business Analytics
• Instant end-of-day summary: total sales, cash collected, digital payments, and estimated gross margin.
• Track your best-selling items, high-profit categories, and dead stock.
• View transaction history with one-tap reversals and audit logs.
• Works with both the Ethiopian Calendar (ዓመተ ምሕረት) and Gregorian Calendar.

🔒 100% Data Privacy & Safe Backups
• Offline-First Guarantee: Your financial records and customer lists remain in the app's local SQLite database unless you enable sync.
• No ads, no third-party tracking, and no forced cloud accounts.
• One-tap database backup: Export your shop database (.sqlite) and share it with a destination you choose.
• Optional Cloud Sync: Connect to our lightweight Telegram bot whenever you want to check shop numbers remotely or collaborate with assistants.

BUILT FOR RELIABILITY:
Runs smoothly on both modern 64-bit devices and older budget 32-bit Android phones (Android 5.0+). Ultra-compact download size (under 25 MB).
